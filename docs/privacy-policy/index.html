Privacy Policy

Meditation Habit Tracker · Effective July 29, 2026

Meditation Habit Tracker is provided by Mathwise ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information handled by the app

The app lets you record and manage:

  • meditation sessions, including dates, durations, and completion status;
  • progress information, such as streaks and practice statistics;
  • app settings, including reminder times and notification preferences; and
  • temporary timer state needed to run an active meditation timer.

This information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives this information, and the app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests access only to its private Google Drive app-data folder. It uses that access to upload, list, restore, and delete backups containing your meditation sessions, progress, and app settings. These backup files are not visible to other apps and are not shared with us.

Google handles authentication and stores the backup files in your Google account. Google may process information needed to provide those services under the Google Privacy Policy. The app stores Google access and refresh tokens in secure device storage so it can perform backups you request and, while you remain signed in, automatic backups of changed data.

Notifications and device permissions

If you enable reminders or use the timer, the app uses Android notification, wake-lock, restart, vibration, and exact-alarm capabilities to deliver reminders and timer alerts. Notification schedules and preferences remain on your device. You can disable notifications through the app or Android settings.

How information is used and shared

Information is used only to provide the app's meditation tracking, timer, reminder, progress, and optional backup features. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. When you enable backup, the relevant information is transmitted to Google Drive at your direction.

Retention and deletion

  • Local information remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • You can delete individual Google Drive backups from the Backup screen. The app keeps up to the latest 20 backups and automatically removes older app backups.
  • Signing out revokes the app's Google access token where possible and removes stored Google tokens from the device. Signing out does not itself delete existing Drive backups.

Security

Data sent to Google services is transmitted over HTTPS, and Google authentication tokens are stored using secure device storage. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Health and wellness notice

Meditation Habit Tracker is a general wellness and habit-tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. We will update the effective date on this page when we do.
